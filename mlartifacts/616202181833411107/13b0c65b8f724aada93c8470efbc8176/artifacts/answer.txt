LLM-as-judge evaluation is a method used in machine learning applications, particularly for Large Language Models (LLMs). It involves using powerful Large Language Models (LLMs) to score outputs from other LLMs at scale. The evaluation system is designed to provide a more accurate evaluation of the performance of LLMs compared to other evaluation metrics, such as Recall and F1-score. It operates through a process of indexing, retrieval, and scoring, which allows for a more nuanced evaluation of the LLMs' performance compared to traditional evaluation metrics.
